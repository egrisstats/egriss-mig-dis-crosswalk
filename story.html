Is every refugee an international migrant?

Scenarios:

Each IRRS category is traced through the IMTM recommendations: did the person change country of residence (an international migrant), or join the resident population another way, by birth, a status change in place or a short stay abroad? Numbered pills mark the non-nested (outlier) cases; hover a route or a paragraph number for detail.

How to read this graph: the IMTM structure and temporary stays IMTM 2025 · Fig. 1, 2, 6

The IMTM structure as the base IMTM 2025 · Fig. 1, 6

IMTM separates two things. International migrants are people who changed their country of residence into the country (the immigration flow, Fig. 1 and para 25 to 27). The resident population is everyone who lives there 12 months or more, split into four groups by birthplace and citizenship (Fig. 6). People can join the resident population through migration, but also through birth, and they can move between groups through naturalisation.

NEST cases: Non-Equivalent Statistical Treatment IRRS 2018 IMTM 2025 IRIS 2020 · cases 16, 17

The misconception: every refugee is an international migrant. In fact IRRS and IMTM are consistent with each other (IRRS paras 77, 80; IMTM paras 34, 53), but one population does not nest inside the other. Each pin is one NEST case with a worked example. All five types are non-nested (outlier) cases; only type 5 is a real misalignment between recommendations.

2 · IRIS categories against the census on internal migration

IRIS 2020 category → What move happened? → P&R Rev. 4 internal migration status

P&R defines internal migrants as people usually resident in a civil division who previously lived outside it; moves within a division are not migration (draft Rev. 4, para 5.64). Results depend on the geography used (major or smaller civil divisions, paras 5.69-5.70) and the measure: place of birth, previous residence and duration, or residence one or five years ago.

IRIS keeps a person in the IDP stock if they cross a border and come back within 12 months without setting up a new usual residence abroad (para 117). Each scenario follows one person over 24 months.
